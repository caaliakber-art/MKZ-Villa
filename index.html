Jinad Residence

Kodungallur · MAAD Concepts design · interactive model

How to use it

  • Bird's-eye: pick a camera angle from the bar, or turn on Spin.
  • Walkthrough: W A S D or arrows to move, drag to look, Space to jump (you can jump onto the plinth and garden edges). Doors are open, and steps lead outside from the sitout, lobby and work area.
  • Upstairs: walk up the stair, or press the Upstairs button.
  • Lights: click any wall switch beside a door, or use the Lights tab.
  • Furniture: open Furniture, turn on Arrange, then drag items, rotate or delete them, or add new ones.

Accuracy

  • Ground floor follows the working drawing (cm dimensions). Use Tools, then Plan overlay, to see the drawing on the floor.
  • Upper floor is indicative. There is no first floor plan yet, so its rooms are not drawn.
  • Your changes are saved in your own browser only.
